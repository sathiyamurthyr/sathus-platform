## 1. Executive Creative Concept: The Connected Intelligence Universe

Instead of separate, decorative 3D objects or isolated floating shapes, Sathus.in is anchored by **an original, monolithic 3D technology sculpture: The Connected Intelligence Universe**.

This sculpture represents the full enterprise engineering lifecycle as an interconnected, living machine:
* **The Central Intelligence Nexus:** A multi-layered glowing core representing centralized governance, deterministic evaluation, and core computing power.
* **Four Organic Architectural Pillars** extending dynamically from the central nexus:
  1. **AI Pillar (Neural Synaptics):** Dendritic nerve pathways, pulsing evaluation nodes, and hyper-dense synaptic clusters.
  2. **Data Pillar (Helical Data Streams):** Fluid, semi-transparent columnar ribbons and optical data packets flowing continuously into the core.
  3. **Cloud Pillar (Multi-Region Cluster Lattice):** Distributed geometric server blocks, orbital ring meshes, and cryptographic perimeter shields.
  4. **Software Pillar (Composable Architectural Matrix):** Modular crystalline blocks and clean domain planes interlocking in real time.

As the user scrolls down the page, **the camera embarks on a continuous cinematic journey through this single sculpture**. Each scroll checkpoint navigates the camera into a specific architectural pillar, transforming the 3D scene in real time to match the editorial narrative.

## 3. Desktop & Mobile Hero Design Specification

### 3.1 Desktop Hero Composition (1440px+ Viewport)

* **Layout Ratio:** 46% Editorial Left / 54% WebGL 3D Right (Seamless full-bleed integration).
* **Left Column Hierarchy:**
  1. **Top Overline:** Frosted capsule pill (`#F9EEF2` fill, `#EDE1E6` hairline border) with pulsing Vibrant Orange `#FF9933` radar dot:  
     `● SATHUS CONNECTED INTELLIGENCE UNIVERSE`
  2. **H1 Headline:** `Instrument Serif`, 76px, line-height `1.05`, tracking `-0.02em`:  
     `Engineering the Future of `  
     `<span class="text-ai-gradient">AI</span>, <span class="text-ai-gradient">Data</span> & Enterprise Software.`
  3. **Lead Paragraph:** `Inter`, 18px / 165% leading, color `#635861`:  
     `We engineer intelligent enterprise platforms, governed data ecosystems and cloud-native applications that turn complex business challenges into verified outcomes.`
  4. **Dual Luxury CTAs:**
     * **Primary Button:** Deep Burgundy `#A20A3A` with gold arrow, subtle shine sweep effect, and -1px lift on hover.
     * **Secondary Button:** Soft White `#FAF8F9` with Orange `#FF9933` calendar icon and hairline border.
  5. **Telemetry Ticker:** Live count-up numbers for `Zero-Trust Architecture`, `99.95% Availability SLA`, and `6 Regulated Industries`.
* **Right Column 3D Viewport:**
  * Width: `640px`, Height: `640px` (aspect square).
  * Canvas is completely transparent (`alpha: true`), rendering seamlessly over the pure white background with subtle ambient radial gradients.
  * **Interactive Floating HUD:**
    * Top Left: `● SATHUS 3D ENGINE • 60 FPS • WEBGL 2.0`
    * Top Right: `[ Reset View ]` button + `[ Drag to Rotate ]` indicator.
    * Bottom Dock: 4 Interactive Pillar Selectors (`[AI Swarms]`, `[Data Lakehouse]`, `[Multi-Cloud]`, `[Composable Apps]`).

### 3.2 Mobile & Tablet Hero Adaptations (320px – 1024px)

* **Tablet (768px – 1024px):**
  * Stacks into a vertical flow: Headline & CTAs at top, followed by a full-width 3D interactive viewport (`500px` height) with touch-drag rotation enabled.
* **Mobile (320px – 767px):**
  * Headline scales to 42px `Instrument Serif`.
  * 3D Viewport runs in **Mobile-Optimized Low-Power Mode**:
    * Particle count reduced from 240 to 60.
    * Pixel ratio locked at `min(window.devicePixelRatio, 1.5)`.
    * Simplified shader passes with zero post-processing overhead.
    * Touch gesture support: Single finger rotates sculpture, double-finger disabled to avoid scroll conflicts.
* **Accessibility / Reduced Motion Mode:**
  * When `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` is active:
    * Auto-rotation and continuous particle drifts are halted.
    * Camera remains fixed at the optimal hero horizon framing.
    * Users can still drag manually if desired, but all continuous animation is paused.

## 4. 3D Asset Specification & Technical Pipeline

### 4.1 Asset Geometry & Topology Budgets

| Component | Geometry Primitive | LOD 0 (Desktop) | LOD 1 (Mobile) | Material Type |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Central Core** | Faceted Icosahedron + Wireframe Dodecahedron | 1,800 tris | 640 tris | `MeshPhysicalMaterial` (Roughness 0.18, Metalness 0.85, Clearcoat 0.6) |
| **Gyroscope Rings** | Dual Segmented Torus | 1,600 tris | 600 tris | `MeshStandardMaterial` (Roughness 0.25, Metalness 0.9, Emissive) |
| **AI Pillar Satellite** | Stellated Octahedron + Orbital Neural Rings | 1,200 tris | 420 tris | `MeshPhysicalMaterial` (Burgundy `#A20A3A`, Emissive `#8C0832`) |
| **Data Pillar Satellite** | Stacked Fluted Cylinders + Ring Ribbons | 1,400 tris | 500 tris | `MeshPhysicalMaterial` (Orange `#FF9933`, Transmission 0.4) |
| **Cloud Pillar Satellite** | Interconnected Chamfered Server Cubes | 1,100 tris | 400 tris | `MeshStandardMaterial` (Warm Orange `#FF9D2E`, Metalness 0.75) |
| **Software Pillar Satellite** | Modular Composable Polyhedron Blocks | 1,300 tris | 450 tris | `MeshPhysicalMaterial` (Burgundy `#A20A3A`, Emissive Accent) |
| **Energy Data Splines** | 3D CatmullRom Bezier Curves | 800 vertices | 300 vertices | `LineDashedMaterial` / Tube Geometry with dynamic offset |
| **Ambient Starfield** | GPU Instanced Point Buffer | 240 points | 60 points | `PointsMaterial` with custom vertex color mapping |
| **TOTAL SCENE BUDGET** | Combined Mesh Geometry | **~9,200 tris** | **~3,370 tris** | **Far below 50k mobile budget (<10MB VRAM)** |

### 4.2 Material & Shader Definitions

* **Burgundy Core Shader:**
  * Base Color: `#A20A3A`
  * Emissive Color: `#8C0832` (Intensity: `0.95`)
  * Roughness: `0.18`, Metalness: `0.85`
  * Clearcoat: `0.6`, Clearcoat Roughness: `0.1`
* **Vibrant Orange Lattice Shader:**
  * Base Color: `#FF9933`
  * Emissive Color: `#FFAE5C` (Intensity: `0.75`)
  * Wireframe: `true`, Wireframe Linewidth: `1.5px`
  * Transparency: `true`, Opacity: `0.82`
* **Data Ribbon Shaders:**
  * CatmullRom 3D bezier curves with custom dash animated uniforms (`material.dashOffset -= delta * speed`).
  * Glowing pulse spheres traveling along bezier arc paths with sinusoidal size breathing.

### 4.3 Scroll Engine & Camera Integration (GSAP ScrollTrigger + Three.js)

* **Scroll Synchronization:**
  * Rather than creating multiple Three.js scenes across sections, **a single persistent Three.js Canvas runs in the background / hero**, controlled by GSAP `ScrollTrigger` timelines.
  * As the user scrolls through page sections, GSAP smoothly interpolates `camera.position`, `camera.rotation`, and `scene.rotation` between Scene 01 and Scene 06 checkpoints.
  * Smooth damping: `scrub: 1.2` with `power2.out` easing prevents abrupt snaps.
* **Viewport Throttling:**
  * `IntersectionObserver` detects when the canvas is in the viewport; if scrolled past Scene 06 into the footer, `requestAnimationFrame` is paused to eliminate idle GPU consumption.
