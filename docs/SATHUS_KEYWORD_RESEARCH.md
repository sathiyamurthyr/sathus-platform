## 1. Research Methodology & Intent Classification

To build sustainable organic visibility and capture qualified enterprise inbound enquiries, keywords are classified into four strategic tiers:

* **Tier 1 — High-Intent Commercial (Transaction / Hire):** Decision-makers searching for enterprise engineering partners, architects, and consultancies.
* **Tier 2 — Enterprise Solution Specific:** Searches evaluating architectural approaches (e.g. Apache Iceberg Lakehouse vs Snowflake, Enterprise Multi-Agent Swarms).
* **Tier 3 — Product & Brand Ecosystem:** Searches seeking specific solutions built by Sathus (OH-ID, Vmaila, Memomes Cloud).
* **Tier 4 — Regulated Industry Authority:** Specific compliance-governed queries (ABDM healthcare integration, HIPAA-compliant document intelligence, PCI-DSS FinTech engineering).

## 3. SERP Feature & AI Overview Optimization Strategy

With the emergence of **Google AI Overviews, Perplexity AI, and SearchGPT**, the website must be grounded for generative answer engines in addition to traditional blue links:

1. **Information Density & Factual Grounding:** Clear, factual definitional paragraphs (`AISummaryBlock`) placed near the top of category landing pages.
2. **Entity Consistency:** Organization name (`Sathus Technology Private Limited`), address, incorporation date (2024-08-28), and official registration numbers maintained consistently across Wikidata, LinkedIn, GitHub, Crunchbase, and Schema.org markup.
3. **Structured Q&A Schema:** Implementation of `FAQPage` and `HowTo` structured data for technical implementation queries (e.g. "How to build an Apache Iceberg lakehouse on Azure").
4. **Primary Source Research:** Thought leadership published on `/resources/insights` and `/resources/blog` featuring verified architecture diagrams and real benchmark telemetry.
